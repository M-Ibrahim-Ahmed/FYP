ScamShield Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

1. Introduction

ScamShield ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our Chrome extension.

2. Data Collection

ScamShield does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information or browsing history.

3. What We Process

To provide security scanning services, we process:

  • URLs: Web addresses are analyzed for phishing threats
  • Page Elements: Links, forms, and images are scanned for malicious patterns
  • Temporary Cache: Scan results are stored locally on your device for 24 hours

Important: No data is permanently stored. All processing is ephemeral and deleted after analysis.

4. How Data is Used

  • URLs are sent to our backend server for threat analysis
  • Analysis includes: blacklist checking, heuristic analysis, and machine learning classification
  • Results are returned to your browser and displayed in the extension
  • No user tracking, profiling, or behavioral analysis is performed

5. Data Storage

  • Local Storage: Scan results are cached locally in your browser for performance (auto-expires after 24 hours)
  • Backend Storage: URLs may be temporarily logged for security analysis but are not linked to any user identity
  • No Permanent Storage: We do not maintain a database of user activity or browsing history

6. Third-Party Services

ScamShield uses the following services:

  • Backend API: Hosted on Railway.app for ML-based threat analysis
  • Screenshot Service: Selenium sandbox for safe URL preview (isolated environment)

No third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services are used.

7. Permissions Explained

ScamShield requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • activeTab: Read page content to scan for threats
  • storage: Store scan results locally for performance (temporary cache)
  • scripting: Inject visual threat warnings on pages
  • notifications: Alert you when dangerous sites are detected
  • tabs: Access current tab URL for analysis
  • <all_urls>: Analyze links on any website you visit

8. Data Security

  • All communication with our backend uses HTTPS encryption
  • Safe preview feature uses an isolated Selenium sandbox (malicious sites never load in your browser)
  • No sensitive data (passwords, cookies, form data) is transmitted

9. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Uninstall the extension at any time
  • Clear local cache via Chrome settings
  • Disable specific features (notifications, etc.)

10. Children's Privacy

ScamShield does not knowingly collect data from users under 13 years of age. The extension is designed for general web browsing security and does not target children.

11. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
